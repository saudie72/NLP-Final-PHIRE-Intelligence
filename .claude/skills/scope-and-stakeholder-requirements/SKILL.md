# scope-and-stakeholder-requirements

**Purpose:** Agree what is being solved, for whom, and what is out of scope.

**Used by:** Product Manager, ERP Business Analyst

**Inputs:** A business problem, enhancement or project phase.

## Steps

1. Identify stakeholders: business owner, affected departments, approvers, support team; note each one's interest and any conflicts.
2. Capture stakeholder requirements as business needs (who needs what, and why), not system features.
3. Write the scope: in scope, out of scope, assumptions, constraints, dependencies.
4. Record open questions and who must sign off.
5. Hand the agreed requirements to erp-functional-analyst for functional specification and user stories.

## Output

Scope statement, stakeholder map and stakeholder requirements list.
