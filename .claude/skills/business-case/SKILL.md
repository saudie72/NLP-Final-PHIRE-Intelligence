# business-case

**Purpose:** Give decision makers a reasoned, transparent case with value and cost side by side.

**Used by:** Product Manager, ERP Manager, ERP Business Analyst

**Inputs:** A recurring issue, enhancement or the application itself, plus volumes from the data.

## Steps

1. State the business problem and who it affects.
2. Measure the current cost from the data where possible: number of tickets, average days open, departments affected.
3. List options (do nothing, workaround, configuration, development) with an effort estimate from the relevant specialist.
4. Estimate benefits (hours saved, tickets avoided, payroll or compliance risk reduced) with stated assumptions.
5. Give a simple ROI or payback and a recommendation.
6. Label every number as measured (from data) or assumed.

## Output

A one-page business case.

## Rules

- Synthetic or user-written example data only; never real PHIRE ticket content (`docs/data-handling.md`).
