# ERP Business Analyst reference notes

## Business analysis practice
- BABOK (IIBA's Guide to the Business Analysis Body of Knowledge) defines the knowledge areas used here:
  elicitation and collaboration, requirements analysis, strategy analysis (current vs future state, business case),
  and solution evaluation.
- Source: https://www.iiba.org/career-resources/a-business-analysis-professionals-foundation-for-success/babok/

## End-to-end processes in PeopleSoft HCM / FSCM
- Hire-to-pay (Core HR → Time and Labor / Absence → Payroll), procure-to-pay (Requisition → PO → Receiving →
  Voucher → Pay Cycle), record-to-report (Journals → Ledger → Close). Each hand-off is a place where tickets cluster.
- Sources: https://docs.oracle.com/cd/F13810_02/hcm92pbr29/eng/hcm, https://docs.oracle.com/en/applications/peoplesoft/human-capital-management/9.2.055/peoplesoft-time-and-labor/integrating-absence-management-time-labor-payroll-north-america.html

## Business case checklist
- Problem, current cost (ticket counts, hours), options, effort, benefits with stated assumptions, ROI, recommendation.
- Recurring issues from the app's clustering and NLTK urgency terms are the evidence base.
