Words that drove VADER's scores (lexicon words found in the tickets):

- positive: help (11), like (10), positive (8), benefit (6), number (5), grant (4), benefits (2), outstanding (2)
- negative: wrong (69), affected (28), discrepancy (26), error (24), errors (15), trouble (11), broken (10), no (10), abend (10), failure (7)

**PeopleSoft lexicon adjustment.** VADER was built for social-media text, and some of its lexicon words are neutral PeopleSoft vocabulary ("pay" in a payroll ticket is not a complaint, "security" in an access request is not praise), while PeopleSoft problem words such as "queued" and "abend" are missing. `nltk_core.PEOPLESOFT_LEXICON` sets 16 domain words to 0 (pay, po, security, save, effective, value, values, support, approved, approval, create, created, attachment, please, rescue, fire) and adds or strengthens problem words (queued -1.5, abend -2.0, abended -2.0, stuck -2.0, outage -2.0, cannot -1.5, incorrect -1.5, discrepancy -1.2, locked -1.0). Words were chosen from the training set and the docs' urgency vocabulary; this demo set was not used to pick them.

| Measure on these tickets | Stock VADER | With PeopleSoft lexicon |
|---|---|---|
| Labels positive / neutral / negative | 122 / 34 / 94 | 13 / 119 / 118 |
| "negative" as an H signal: precision / recall | 0.37 / 0.40 | 0.33 / 0.45 |

**Finding.** The adjustment removes most false "positive" labels on routine requests, so VADER now describes tone more plausibly, but it does not make VADER a better priority signal: recall for H rose slightly and precision fell. The app therefore shows VADER as descriptive tone only; the urgency vocabulary below is the stronger signal, and the rule-based priority suggestion is unchanged.

## spaCy: EntityRuler for PeopleSoft objects

With the EntityRuler added before the statistical NER, 85% of tickets mention at least one PeopleSoft module, page, process or tool. What `en_core_web_sm` alone calls those same spans:

| en_core_web_sm label for PeopleSoft objects | Count |
|---|---|
| (not an entity) | 176 |
| ORG | 123 |
| PERSON | 18 |
| GPE | 8 |
| FAC | 5 |
| WORK_OF_ART | 3 |

**Finding.** The general-purpose model has never seen PeopleSoft: it labels pages and processes as ORG, PERSON, PRODUCT or misses them entirely. The EntityRuler fixes this with rules from the research docs. Remaining errors are terms outside the pattern list and capitalised business words the model still tags (see below).

## Limitations

- Synthetic, template-generated text: real tickets will be noisier and VADER and the EntityRuler will miss more.
- The EntityRuler only knows the terms listed in `pipeline/patterns.py`; new pages and processes need new patterns.
- spaCy runs only at build time (it cannot run in the browser), so spaCy results cover the demo dataset; NLTK also runs live in the browser through Pyodide for any text.
